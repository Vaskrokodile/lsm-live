### Data and recipe

The add-on corpus contains 49,502 unique WebSight v0.2 pages (44,552 train; 4,950 validation), disjoint from the earlier v3a WebSight shard exclusion. The split and packed reason-label sidecars were hash-verified. WebSight attribution: HuggingFaceM4, [WebSight dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceM4/WebSight), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); Hugo Laurençon, Léo Tronchon & Victor Sanh, [“Unlocking the conversion of Web Screenshots into HTML Code with the WebSight Dataset”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09029). The dataset card also requires compliance with original source-content licenses and disclosure of WebSight use when releasing a model or application.

Ultron, learning rate `1e-4`, betas `(0.9, 0.984095744256)`, factored-matrix `beta1=0`, batch 8 × 512 bytes, bf16, TF32 enabled, eager loss (not compiled), auxiliary weight `0.3`, chunk size 32. The 8.192M new tokens are about 0.089 nominal token-count equivalent of the 92.6M-byte training split; this is not an epoch.

The final closure ratio rose versus the start, while HTML/body pair counts fell and doctype remained absent; the best checkpoint did not improve these metrics. All samples were truncated at the 768-byte limit. This is a small stochastic structural sample—not robust evidence of improvement, rendered-page acceptance, or convergence. The model is **not converged on HTML coding**.
